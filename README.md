# dano-airline-passenger-satisfaction-analysis
Passenger Satisfaction Analysis for Dano Airlines using Microsoft Power BI

Dano Airlines | Passenger Satisfaction Analysis

✈️ Project Overview

Dano Airlines experienced a significant decline in passenger satisfaction, with the latest survey showing satisfaction had fallen below 50% for the first time. Management needed to understand the root causes of customer dissatisfaction and identify the areas that required immediate improvement.

As the Data Analyst assigned to this project, I analyzed passenger survey data and developed an interactive Power BI dashboard to uncover satisfaction drivers, identify performance gaps, and provide data-driven recommendations to improve the overall customer experience.

⸻

🎯 Business Problem

Dano Airlines wanted answers to the following business questions:

* Why has passenger satisfaction dropped below 50%?
* Which service areas contribute most to customer dissatisfaction?
* Which customer segments are least satisfied?
* How does satisfaction vary across travel classes?
* What operational improvements should management prioritize to increase passenger satisfaction?

⸻

📊 Dataset

The dataset contains over 120,000 passenger survey records collected from airline customers.

It includes information such as:

* Passenger Satisfaction
* Customer Type
* Travel Class
* Type of Travel
* Service Ratings
* Flight Delays
* In-flight Experience
* Booking Experience
* Demographic Information

⸻

🛠 Tools Used

* Power BI
* Power Query
* DAX
* Data Modeling
* Data Visualization

⸻

📈 Dashboard Features

The dashboard enables management to:

* Monitor overall passenger satisfaction.
* Compare satisfaction across travel classes.
* Analyze customer satisfaction by customer type.
* Evaluate airline service performance.
* Measure the impact of arrival delays.
* Filter results by Customer Type, Travel Class, and Type of Travel.

⸻

🔍 Key Findings

The analysis revealed several important insights:

* Overall passenger satisfaction is 43.4%, which is below the company’s target.
* Economy Class passengers recorded the lowest satisfaction levels.
* Returning customers reported higher satisfaction than first-time passengers.
* In-flight Wi-Fi received the lowest service rating, followed by online booking and gate location.
* Operational delays continue to negatively impact passenger satisfaction.
* Core service quality varies considerably across different service touchpoints, highlighting areas that require immediate improvement.

⸻

💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, Dano Airlines should:

* Improve the Economy Class travel experience.
* Upgrade the in-flight Wi-Fi service.
* Simplify and improve the online booking process.
* Reduce operational and arrival delays.
* Improve airport gate information and passenger communication.
* Strengthen the onboarding experience for first-time passengers.
* Continuously monitor customer satisfaction using an interactive dashboard.

⸻

📌 Business Impact

This dashboard provides management with a centralized view of passenger satisfaction, enabling faster decision-making and helping prioritize investments that will improve customer experience, increase passenger loyalty, and strengthen the airline’s competitive position.
